# Supporto / Support

Use this repository's Issues for reproducible problems or suggestions.

Include:

- TommeView and Windows versions;
- what you expected and what happened;
- exact reproduction steps;
- video codec, resolution and frame rate when relevant;
- a screenshot with private information removed, if helpful.

Non pubblicare filmati riservati, annotazioni personali, cookie, credenziali o URL privati.
Do not publish confidential footage, personal annotations, cookies, credentials or private URLs.

This is an independently maintained project, without a guaranteed response time.
Donations are voluntary and do not purchase priority support.
