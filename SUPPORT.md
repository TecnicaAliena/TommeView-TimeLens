# Support

Use this repository's [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/TecnicaAliena/TommeView-TimeLens/issues)
for reproducible problems, feature requests and improvement suggestions.

Include:

- the TommeView and Windows versions;
- what you expected and what happened;
- exact reproduction steps;
- video codec, resolution and frame rate when relevant; and
- a screenshot with private information removed, if helpful.

Do not publish confidential footage, personal annotations, cookies, credentials
or private URLs.

This is an independently maintained project, without a guaranteed response
time. Donations are voluntary and do not purchase priority support.
